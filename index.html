Guarantee Basic requires a lower investment and includes a KWA budget, making it suitable for maintaining a basic presence and generating opportunities at an entry level. Verified Supplier Basic costs more but delivers clear differences in manufacturer verification, Star Direct level, RFQ allowance, showcase capacity, operating tools and visibility in specialised Alibaba.com sections.

For SB TECH, the objective is not only to renew the subscription but also to reinforce buyer confidence. As more Vietnamese factories join cross-border e-commerce platforms, competition for keywords, advertising costs and access to high-quality buyers will continue to increase. Having operated the store for one year and maintaining Verified status allows SB TECH to accumulate search ranking, buyer behaviour data and factory credibility while competitive density remains relatively low.

02 / ANSWERS FROM THE MEETING

Questions raised by SB TECH

01

If SB TECH subscribes to the GGS package first and later upgrades to Verified Supplier after a few months, how will payment and the contract term be handled?

When SB TECH upgrades to Verified Supplier, Alibaba.com will sign a new contract. The term of the new contract starts from the date Business Verification (BV) is completed for the Verified Supplier package.

For the existing GGS contract, the period already used will be reconciled. The fee corresponding to the unused period will be refunded or handled in accordance with the policy applicable at the time of upgrade.

Example: SB TECH renews its store in October 2026 and completes Business Verification (BV) on 5 November 2026. In January 2027, SB TECH decides to upgrade to Verified Supplier. The applicable fee will be the membership fee of the Verified Supplier package selected at the time of upgrade, and the new contract term starts from the date BV is completed for the new package, for example 5 January 2027.

The 12-month GGS term is counted from BV on 5 November 2026. The service period runs from that date to BV for the upgraded package on 5 January 2027, equivalent to two months used. The remaining ten months will be refunded in accordance with the applicable policy.

Note: Customers renewing after 31 December 2026 will be charged at the new rate, which is higher than the current price.
02

Who will support SB TECH in operating its Alibaba.com store?

Kin Khiêm is currently the Account Manager for SB TECH and will directly support the company throughout the operation of its Alibaba.com store.

For specialised matters, Kin will coordinate with the relevant Alibaba.com teams, including Trade Assurance, Logistics and Marketing Ads, as well as other related departments. The objective is to ensure that each issue is received and handled by the right specialist team.

03

SB TECH would like a training session on Trade Assurance to have related questions answered. How will this be supported?

After SB TECH completes the renewal of its Alibaba.com store, both parties will arrange a working session with the Trade Assurance team. The session can be held online, subject to the availability of both parties.

The Trade Assurance team will advise on and answer SB TECH's questions regarding Trade Assurance processes, conditions and operating procedures.

03 / COORDINATION APPROACH

Ensuring issues are received and routed to the right team

During operation, SB TECH may contact the Account Manager directly for initial guidance. For each specialised topic, the Account Manager will coordinate with the relevant Alibaba.com team to provide an appropriate response.

Coordination principles: receive the issue, identify the right specialist team, agree the discussion schedule and respond based on verified information.
